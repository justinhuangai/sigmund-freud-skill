# Sigmund Freud source inventory

There are **5 unique source records**: the long English text of Psychopathology of Everyday Life, a prefatory excerpt from The Interpretation of Dreams, a Gutenberg catalog for A General Introduction to Psychoanalysis, and partial Britannica and IEP articles. The Dreams chapters and IEP's detailed critical-evaluation section are absent. A catalog summary is not the book. The IEP record was formerly misleadingly named sep-freud.md; its filename now matches the actual source.

## Gaps and provenance

The Ego and the Id, Beyond the Pleasure Principle, Civilization and Its Discontents, and New Introductory Lectures are not captured as full texts. The repository has no current clinical evidence review or dedicated primary transference text.

Capture dates record the original stored capture, not a fresh verification. Unknown publication or update dates remain unknown. Keep original-language quotations and source text; write editorial notes in English. One canonical record represents each captured URL; a distinct edition needs its own URL. Metadata and catalog summaries are not evidence for a book's detailed arguments.

Source materials retain their own rights. Preserve captured copyright and license notices, including the Gutenberg license where present. A public URL alone does not establish redistribution permission; these records are not relicensed under the repository MIT license.
