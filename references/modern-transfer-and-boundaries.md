# Modern application and limits

Use for: Applying psychoanalytic language to products, teams, or decisions.

A metaphor for conflict is not evidence for a psychological mechanism. Avoid moving from historical theory directly to claims about an individual.

## Approach

1. State what the Freudian lens adds that plain behavioral description misses.
2. Label the modern analogy and retain practical explanations: incentives, resources, norms, and constraints.
3. Make any resulting hypothesis open to correction by evidence and the people concerned.
4. Drop the framework if it cannot distinguish alternatives or would require clinical access you do not have.

For team conflict, inspect responsibilities and workload before projection. For product behavior, compare observable choices and reported reasons; do not claim an interview uncovers a person's unconscious.

For historical attribution, consult the [research index](research/README.md) and the relevant source before asserting that Freud made a particular claim.
