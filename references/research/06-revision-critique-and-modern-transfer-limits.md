# Revision, critique, and modern limits

## Evidence status

The IEP introduction flags debate about scientific status; its detailed critical-evaluation section is absent. This repository does not contain a current clinical evidence review.

## Empirical criticism

Historical influence, plausibility, and testable evidence are different standards. Verify current empirical claims from appropriate research rather than using a historical case story as proof.

## Sex and reductionism

Do not reduce every choice to sex, childhood, or aggression. State which theoretical period and work is under discussion when explaining Freud's changing framework; general cultural familiarity is not a citation.

## Interpretive flexibility

A story that explains every outcome, including its own rejection, cannot distinguish alternatives. State what could weaken a proposed explanation and accept that evidence may remain insufficient.

## Modern transfer

For product or team questions, examine friction, incentives, resources, and reported reasons. Psychological analogy can suggest questions, but not justify diagnosis, covert manipulation, or dismissal of a complaint.
