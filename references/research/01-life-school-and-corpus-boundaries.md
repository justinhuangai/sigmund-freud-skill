# Life, school, and corpus boundaries

## Evidence status

The two reference excerpts provide partial biography. One complete-looking historical English text, one prefatory excerpt, and one catalog do not constitute Freud's collected works.

## Life and formation

The reference captures situate Freud in medicine and neurology, with Charcot and Breuer among his influences. Use these as a historical starting point, not a substitute for checking a specific event or disputed date.

## Psychoanalysis as a movement

Distinguish Freud's work from later psychoanalytic schools and from the skill's contemporary analogies. A later defense taxonomy or technique is not automatically Freud's own formulation.

## Corpus and editions

Everyday Life is the 1914 English edition translated by Brill. Its introduction explicitly notes modified or substituted examples. Dreams is only prefatory matter here, and the Introductory Lectures record is a catalog.

## Textual gaps

No local full text of The Ego and the Id, Beyond the Pleasure Principle, Civilization and Its Discontents, or New Introductory Lectures is included. Do not quote these from memory as if the repository had verified the passages.
