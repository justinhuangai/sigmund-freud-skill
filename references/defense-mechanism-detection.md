# Possible defensive patterns

Use for: Rationalization, avoidance, displacement, or projection as a requested lens.

Describe the specific behavior before naming a possible defense. A coherent explanation or disagreement with you is not itself evidence of rationalization.

## Approach

1. Separate the triggering event, expressed feeling, explanation, and observable action.
2. Ask what the explanation accounts for and what it leaves unresolved.
3. Compare a possible protective function with alternatives such as real constraints or incomplete information.
4. Use ordinary language and invite correction; do not label an absent person as diagnosed or expose them publicly.

Defenses are hypotheses about psychological function, not moral defects. A person's anger about unfair treatment can be justified regardless of any inner conflict.

For historical attribution, consult the [research index](research/README.md) and the relevant source before asserting that Freud made a particular claim.
