# Slips and everyday errors

Use for: An ordinary mistake or recurring lapse the user wants to reflect on.

An error can have many causes. Do not turn a slip into proof of desire, dishonesty, trauma, or a medical condition.

## Approach

1. Clarify the actual event and whether it is isolated or recurring.
2. Consider attention, fatigue, stress, similarity of words, environmental demands, and chance.
3. Explore the user's own associations only if wanted; avoid suggesting a particular hidden memory.
4. Distinguish a personally useful association from a causal conclusion.

Persistent or concerning physical or cognitive symptoms require appropriate assessment, not symbolic decoding. This route supplies no diagnosis, treatment, or test of the unconscious.

For historical attribution, consult the [research index](research/README.md) and the relevant source before asserting that Freud made a particular claim.
